Our Evolution

A Decade of Driving Digital Excellence

2012-2025: From Humble Beginnings to Global Influence

2012

The journey began, specialising in WordPress and Magento, planting our roots in the digital solution space. As a small but highly ambitious team, we focused on helping local businesses establish their first online presence. We quickly realized the immense potential of custom e-commerce solutions and dedicated ourselves to mastering digital storefronts, laying a solid foundation for the technical expertise that defines us today.

2013-2014

Our swift expansion into web, mobile, and e-commerce solutions doubled our team and marked significant revenue growth. We started taking on complex custom application development, moving beyond basic CMS platforms. By establishing a dedicated mobile app division, we empowered our clients to reach their customers directly on iOS and Android, setting new internal records for project scale and complexity.

2015-2016

Started incorporating advanced frameworks and modern UI methodologies, allowing us to handle robust enterprise-grade applications. We fully embraced modern JavaScript ecosystems like React and Angular, transitioning from traditional web design to highly interactive single-page applications (SPAs). This period was defined by an obsession with performance, seamless user experiences, and scalable backend architectures.

2018

Global expansion began. We started securing international clientele, building our strong reputation in the global IT ecosystem. By delivering consistently high-quality software to partners in North America, Europe, and Australia, we transformed from a local agency into a globally recognized technology partner. We established rigorous quality assurance protocols and agile workflows to support our growing international footprint.

2019-2020

Adopted remote-first architectures, successfully delivering high-impact projects across multiple time zones during the global shift. As the world transitioned to digital-first, we were perfectly positioned to help traditional businesses undergo rapid digital transformation. We scaled our cloud infrastructure capabilities and helped dozens of enterprises migrate their operations online without skipping a beat.

2021-2023

Introduced AI integrations and advanced cloud solutions into our core services, keeping our clients ahead of the technology curve. We built specialized teams focusing on machine learning algorithms, natural language processing, and data automation. This allowed us to upgrade our clients' legacy systems into smart, predictive platforms that drastically reduced their operational costs and skyrocketed efficiency.

2024

A record-breaking year with over 500+ successful project deliveries and the official launch of our premium digital marketing wing. Realizing that great software needs great visibility, we brought top-tier SEO and performance marketing experts in-house. This allowed us to offer a true end-to-end solution—not just building extraordinary platforms, but ensuring they dominate search engines and drive massive revenue.

2025

Continuing our mission to drive digital excellence, leveraging emerging tech to empower modern businesses globally. We are currently pioneering next-generation web3 integrations and hyper-personalized AI experiences. Our vision for the future remains clear: to be the most innovative, reliable, and results-driven technology partner for ambitious brands ready to scale to the next level.
